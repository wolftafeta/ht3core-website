How to get access

HT3Core runs two regions, NA and EU. Use the in-game globe (region switcher) to move between them.

  1. 1

    Join and link

    Join our Discord, then run /link with the code you get on the server to connect Minecraft and Discord.

  2. 2

    Verify

    Run /verify. Players with HT3 or higher on PvPTiers/MCTiers get instant access.

  3. 3

    Or boost the Discord

    Only 50 boosters hold a slot at once. When it is full you wait in the waitlist, and when a booster stops, their slot goes to the first in line.
