Linking

  • Step 1: join the server and type /link in game. You get a short code.
  • Step 2: in our Discord, type /link and enter the code.
  • You get: your Minecraft name and rank on Discord, your roles kept in sync, and access to linked-only features such as HT3+ tier roles.

HT3+ verification

  • Automatic: when you join the server, your HT3+ tiers are checked for you.
  • Fallback: if nothing was found, run /verify to check again.
  • Discord: once verified and linked, you receive your tier roles in Discord.

Boosters

  • Boost the Discord server to get the Booster rank in game and a Booster role on Discord.
  • Link your account (see above) so the server knows who you are. Perks apply while your boost is active.
  • Ask in the Discord if your perks do not show up after linking.

  • Each region has its own separate ranked ladder. Use the NA / EU switch on the Ranked, Leaderboards and Matches pages to view one.
  • Your profile shows combined stats: for each kit, your best region counts, and overall Points are the sum of those.

Server ranks

Win ranked duels to earn Points. Every tier up to Master is split into three divisions (I-III) of equal size. Points are tracked per kit, plus an overall total.

  • First game: you start at 0 Points. Win your first ranked game in a kit and you are Silver, lose and you are Bronze.
  • Overall Points are the sum of your Points in every kit.
  • Champion: the #1 player of each kit, plus one overall.
  • Unranked queues do not change your Points.
