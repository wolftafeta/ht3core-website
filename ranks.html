Ranks

Win ranked duels to gain ELO. Every tier up to Master is split into three divisions (I-III) of equal size. Ranked ELO is tracked per kit, plus an overall rating.

How it works

  • First game: you start at 1000 ELO. Win your first ranked game in a kit and you are Silver, lose and you are Bronze.
  • Champion: the #1 player (highest ELO) of each kit, among players with at least one ranked game. There is one Champion per kit and one overall.
  • Unranked queues do not change your ELO.

Commands

/rankedOpen the ranked queue menu
/elo [player] [kit]Show ranked ratings
/stats [player]Open a player's stats and profile (alias /profile)
/rankOpen the ranked tier ladder: all tiers with their ELO ranges, the current Champion per kit and your own tier
/linkLink your Minecraft account with Discord
/leaderboardThe duels leaderboards (aliases /lb, /top)
